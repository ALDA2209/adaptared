Roadmap

  1. Pilot in Huánuco

    First 12 communities with SENAMHI data and local reports. The model is validated against past events.

  2. Central jungle

    Neighboring regions are added. Alerts by SMS and radio for areas without stable internet.

  3. Andean-Amazon region

    Hundreds of communities with municipal subscriptions and partnerships with agricultural insurers.
